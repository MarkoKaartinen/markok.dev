How AI has been used on this site.
{{< /lead >}}

Let's start with the most important thing. Content has **not** been created with AI. Blog articles and other content are all my own work. Photos are either taken by me or sourced from places I try to remember to credit. If something happens to be fully AI-generated, there will be a note saying so.

I cannot guarantee that external links are not made with AI. I link interesting articles and things from the internet.

AI has been used to help with translated articles from my [finnish blog](https://markokaartinen.net).

## So how has AI been used then

AI has been used as an assistant though. I might brainstorm with AI on an article or ask for tips and pointers. The actual text I write myself (and it usually shows 😆).

AI has been help to make some modifications to the site. This site is made with Hugo and I sometime need some help with using and modifying the site. If AI has done something, I've reviewed and edited it as needed, and naturally tested it too.

I wrote about this in more detail on the blog and you can read that article [here](/blog/2026/how-i-use-ai).
